# Third-party credits and notices

Distribution review dated 16 November 2026: PyFracPaQ is an independently maintained port with its own modifications, licensed under MIT; third-party licences remain applicable. The [scope, evidence and current treatment](docs/licenses/README.md) and [inventory with URLs and SHA-256 hashes](docs/licenses/manifest.json) supplement the historical records below. The public ZIP contains application source code and runtime resources, without the original MATLAB distribution or dependency binaries. Older `*-source.zip` files are internal reference archives and must not be published.

The contracts and technical reports cited below are maintenance material from the full repository and may be absent from the usage ZIP. Their links point to GitHub and require an internet connection. The licences and attributions for the distributed application and resources remain available offline in this file, LICENSE and the notices in pyfracpaq/resources.

The examples/SD11A.txt example accompanies the manual as an unchanged copy of SD11A.txt from the FracPaQ MATLAB 2.8 distribution supplied by the maintainer. The FracPaQ Contributors/David Healy and collaborators attribution and the distribution's MIT notice are retained. Its checksum and provenance are recorded in examples/provenance.json. Manual illustrations are Python results or interface screenshots, with sources recorded in docs/manual/figure-provenance.json; corrected results from the port are not attributed to MATLAB.

This record was started in Sprint 0 and reviewed during the S16 audit. Preserve the full notices when porting or redistributing excerpts; do not attribute exclusive authorship to the Python port.

In Sprint 2, FracPaQlogo.jpeg and FracPaQicon.jpeg were copied unchanged into pyfracpaq/resources. Their origins, SHA-256 hashes and MIT attribution are recorded in pyfracpaq/resources/provenance.json. The ui_spec.json control specification derives from the preserved FIG file and records its checksum. The package includes these resources and the root notices; it does not include font files.

## S16 audit and local distribution

The 0.1.0rc1 candidate includes this file's notices and LICENSE in the wheel; dependencies retain their own licences in the wheelhouse wheels. No Helvetica/Arial font or MATLAB runtime is redistributed. Native captures are test results, with versions/fonts/hashes recorded; they do not incorporate proprietary toolbox implementations into the port. Hough corrections mathematically reimplement quantisation/suppression/ordering contracts. FracPaQ 2.8 remains the reference and retains the credits above. Q05 censoring was explicitly approved by the user in S16; do not attribute its corrected results to the original behaviour.

The continuation of the S16 audit adds elementary marker and grid materials (`matlab_markers.npz`/`matlab_grids.npz`). They were generated from independent graphical scenes without fracture data and frozen by `freeze_native_markers.py`/`freeze_native_grids.py`; their JSON files record origin, version and SHA-256. These materials contain neither cropped scientific figures nor MATLAB code. Scientific reference figures remain separate from application resources. Multisample composition and the TeX layout are Python implementations; Computer Modern symbols are obtained from the free files already distributed by Matplotlib, with their notices preserved in the dependency. No MathWorks font was copied or incorporated into the package.
